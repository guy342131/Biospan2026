About the symposium

A national gathering at the intersection of biology's fastest-moving fields

BIOSPAN 2026 brings together researchers, clinicians, and students working across genetics, neurobiology, immunology, and the broader biological sciences, for three days of plenaries, contributed talks, posters, and cross-disciplinary conversation — hosted by the Department of Bioscience and Biomedical Engineering at IIT Bhilai.

Four tracks

Scientific themes

Genetics & Genomics

Molecular variation, sequencing, and genome-scale biology.

Neurobiology & Neurodegenerative Disorders

Circuits, cognition, and the biology of neurological disease.

Immunology & Metabolic Disorders

Host defence, endocrinology, and metabolic dysregulation.

Advances in Biological Sciences

Emerging methods and cross-cutting biological questions.
